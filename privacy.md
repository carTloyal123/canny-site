# Canny Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 30, 2026_

Canny is a document scanner that works entirely on your device. This policy is short because
there is very little to say.

## What we collect

Nothing. Canny has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no
third-party code. The app does not connect to any server run by us or by anyone else.

## Your documents

Scanning, text recognition, image enhancement and search all run on your iPhone or iPad. Your
scans are saved as ordinary PDF files in the place you choose in Settings:

- **On This Device:** the app's own folder, protected by iOS file encryption.
- **iCloud (App Only)** or **iCloud Drive:** your own iCloud account. Apple stores and syncs
  these files under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We never
  receive them.

Deleting a scan in Canny deletes its file. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the
device. Files in iCloud stay there until you delete them.

## Camera and photos

Canny uses the camera only while you are scanning. When you import photos, you pick them with
the system photo picker, so Canny sees only the photos you choose. Neither ever leaves your
device through Canny.

## Children

Canny collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
