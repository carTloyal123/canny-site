# Canny Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue at
[github.com/carTloyal123/canny-site/issues](https://github.com/carTloyal123/canny-site/issues).
Please don't attach scans of private documents.

## Common questions

**Where are my scans?**
Each scan is a PDF. In **On This Device** mode, open the Files app and go to
On My iPhone › Canny. In **iCloud Drive** mode, they are in iCloud Drive › Canny.
**iCloud (App Only)** keeps them inside Canny on all your devices.

**How do I move my scans to or from iCloud?**
Open Settings in Canny and pick a storage location. Canny moves your existing scans for you.

**Why does a scan say it might be blurry or too dark?**
Canny checks every page for blur, glare, low light and blank pages. Rescan in better light,
or keep the page if it is readable. The blur check adapts to your camera over your first
several scans.

**Does Canny need an internet connection?**
No. Everything, including text recognition, works offline.

**Is my data shared with anyone?**
No. See the [privacy policy](privacy).
